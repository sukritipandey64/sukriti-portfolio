## Stack
- Vite multi-page static site, vanilla HTML/CSS/JS. Pages are registered in `vite.config.js` (`rollupOptions.input`).
- Lenis smooth scroll (lerp 0.16 desktop; native scroll on touch).
- Fonts self-hosted via @fontsource (Cormorant Garamond 300/400 + italics, Syne 400/500). Google Fonts is blocked in the build sandbox.
- `src/styles/base.css` — tokens + shared components. `home.css`, `case.css` — page styles.
- `src/js/site.js` — cursor, reveals, glow cards, room lighting (`data-light` → `LOOKS` map), scroll-driven
  background "camera" (zoom + pan + sway), gallery walk over the one image (`.gallery-fx`: a track-light beam + pool per room on alternating walls, partition shadows sweeping past at room boundaries, dust motes lit by the beams, a slight camera turn per room; off under reduced motion), lightbox for `[data-zoom]`.
- `src/js/motion.js` — signature motion: hero letters scatter then snap ("Chaos, but curated"), Velocity film on the homepage work card (product-video style: real screens, camera zooms, cursor, captions, clickable chapters; autoplays in view, pause button; scene script lives in motion.js `SHOTS`), sketch→screen scrub on Velocity (inside the Final design tabs; scroll-driven until dragged; range input for keyboard), tasting-note decode on Digital Barista, and the homepage Ctrl+Alt+Decaf card (calm: one example menu line at a time, then its plain-words version and a bitterness dot scale; dots to switch lines; lots of space on purpose). Case study covers fade a product visual in full-bleed (`.cover-bleed`): the dashboard on Velocity, three lo-fi phones on Barista. All off under prefers-reduced-motion. One signature moment per page.
- Case studies share a section index (progress bar, right-edge dot rail on desktop, bottom chip on touch) built from `[data-toc]` in site.js, an at-a-glance strip, read time, and a Next case study card.
- The Curator (`curator.html`, `src/js/curator.js`, `src/styles/curator.css`; linked from the homepage Installation plaque): a phone-first game
  (full-screen app on a phone, a phone frame beside the copy on desktop; site nav/footer hidden on phone). Three rooms played in order
  (`ROOMS`, `MATERIALS`: gallery wall / editorial spread / product screen), five pieces each, a three-line brief judged live. Hooks are deliberate:
  a card with three hidden lines on the home screen, rooms that unlock in order, a speed bonus that drains, snap streaks, and a card to keep.
  Each room reveals one card line (Pace: leap/look, Method: edit/commit, Eye: big/small first; `traitFor`); pace x method gives the type (`TYPES`).
  Score = 100 per brief line + snaps (streak, capped) + remaining speed bonus (only once two lines are met). The finished card (name optional)
  downloads as a 1080x1350 PNG drawn on canvas (`drawCard`), with Web Share where supported. Progress in localStorage (`curator-v2`, optional).
- Editorial edition (EXPLORATION, noindex, not linked from the dark site): `editorial.html`, `editorial-velocity.html`, `editorial-decaf.html`,
  `editorial-collection.html`, all generated by `python3 scripts/build_editorial.py`; look in `src/styles/editorial.css`, reveals in `src/js/editorial.js`
  (standalone: no base.css/site.js). Light paper/ink/vermilion, Bodoni Moda. Copy is short and a little witty on purpose (her brief). The editor's letter carries the work narrative (see homepage section 03 rule): no project list or numbers. Same content rules
  as the main site. The collection page is typographic plates (no artwork images: only hang images that are free to use or her own photos);
  two entries are hers (Arsham x Tiffany box, the SFMOMA piece whose title she forgot), three are placeholders she may swap.
- Homepage section 03 "The work I do" (`.story`) and the editorial editor's letter are a short first-person NARRATIVE of the kind of work she does and enjoys. Her rule: the portfolio must not show her resume. No project lists, counts, big figures or resume links in that section (the resume download stays in Contact only).
- Two editions, one portfolio (her idea, "two sides of me"): Chaos = the dark gallery, Curated = the editorial. Every page has a twin and a fixed
  `.edition` switch (bottom-left) injected by site.js / editorial.js from the `TWINS` map: `/`↔`/editorial.html`, `/velocity-ai.html`↔`/editorial-velocity.html`,
  `/digital-barista.html`↔`/editorial-decaf.html`, `/collection.html`↔`/editorial-collection.html`. The dark collection (`collection.html`, `src/styles/collection.css`)
  is also written by `scripts/build_editorial.py` (`PLATES`). The Curator is its own app, shared by both. Which edition is the default at `/` is HER decision
  (pending): the dark one still is. Keep facts identical across twins.
- Picture rule (both editions): an image AND its caption fit one screen on a MacBook Air (~680-760px tall): editorial `.fig img` max-height
  `calc(100svh - 12rem)`; dark `.shot img` `calc(100svh - 13rem)`.
- Images in `public/images/` (atmosphere.jpg, velocity/*.jpg). Resume at `public/files/Sukriti-Pandey-Resume.pdf`.

## Spacing (one rhythm everywhere)
- Use the tokens in base.css: `--room-y` (section top/bottom), `--gap-label` (eyebrow -> heading), `--gap-head`
  (heading -> content), `--gap-block` (block -> block), `--gap-para`. No ad-hoc margins between sections or blocks.

## Legibility floor
- Small uppercase text >= 0.74rem; amber text uses solid `--amber-soft` (#E0C49C); small text gets the dark halo
  text-shadow defined in base.css / case.css. Keep these when adding new text styles.
