03 — The work I do

I joined as a developer
and kept asking why.

Why a process lived in six inboxes. Why the tool everyone needed was a spreadsheet nobody trusted. At some point I was allowed to fix the why, and that became the job.

So now I take the messy, manual thing a team has quietly put up with and turn it into something they open without being told to. I design it, direct the build, and stay after launch, because the interesting part starts when real people get their hands on it.

What I enjoy most is the question underneath every interface: what makes someone actually finish, adopt, or change how they work?

It’s why I build learning games people complete on purpose. And it’s why my Fridays go to helping colleagues, most of them nowhere near tech, find their own uses for AI.
