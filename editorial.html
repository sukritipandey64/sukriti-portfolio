01 Editor’s letter

My brain does not open one tab.

I explore wildly, then build the map. Psychology drew me to UX. Architecture showed me how systems hold together. Fashion taught me that restraint is its own kind of intelligence.

I’m a product designer who takes work from research to production: four and a half years shipping internal products at Deloitte, where I design the thing, direct the AI-assisted build, and stay with it after launch.

Four and a half years of work that shipped.

Internal tools, so there are no screens to show. Read these as the numbers page.

  1. 60+people, every day

    Project tracker

    Replaced a team’s spreadsheets and status calls. Still live, and still mine to run.

    SharePoint, Figma, an AI-drafted PRD
  2. 130k+unique visitors

    Intranet hub

    Content scattered across old sites, given a structure people could actually navigate.

    Information architecture across eight sites
  3. 40h→10hper microsite

    Design-to-production pipeline

    Figma straight to deployable code. The AI builds it; I direct it, debug it and ship it.

    Figma Make, MCP servers, hand-checked HTML
  4. 178+requests managed

    Review platform

    A process that lived in email threads, now one place where every request has an owner.

    Role-based interfaces, multi-document flows
  5. ~40hbuild time saved

    Gamified learning

    Training people finish because they want to, not because a deadline is coming.

    Octalysis, taken from concept to code
  6. 0→1a new team service

    UX, as an offering

    UX wasn’t a service on my team. Now people ask for it by name.
