The collection · Hung, not ranked

Five things I can’t stop looking at.

Not all of it is art. Some of it is a roof. All of it changed how I look at a screen.

01

No. 01

Bronze Eroded Tiffany Blue Box

Daniel Arsham × Tiffany & Co.

Sculpture · 2021

The most recognisable box in retail, shown as if it had been dug up centuries from now. It breaks the box, literally. Packaging as fossil.

02

No. 02

Olympiapark roof

Frei Otto with Behnisch & Partner

Architecture · Munich · 1972

A stadium roof that behaves like a tent. Structure doing the least it can and getting away with it.

03

No. 03

Sagrada Família

Antoni Gaudí

Architecture · Barcelona · begun 1882

Columns that branch like trees, light that arrives in colour. Also the world’s most patient deadline.

04

No. 04

SFMOMA, the rippled façade

Snøhetta

Architecture · San Francisco · 2016

A building that looks like fog had a good idea. Quiet from far away, restless up close.

05

No. 05

Untitled (I forgot the title)

Seen at SFMOMA

Name misplaced · impression intact

One piece stopped me in the gallery and I didn’t photograph the label. Rookie curator error. This wall is holding its place.
